## DEFENSE BOT 3000

## DESCRIPTION
Have you ever wanted a bot that can play some serious robot soccer defense? Never forget which team it's on? 
Always scores on a near-perfect offense? Well then THIS robot is exactly what you AREN'T looking for!
By super-generalizing the code to work on both teams, the robot tends to forget who it's playing for. Yes, really.
The offense is mostly a prayer that the ball will go into the goal like a poorly delivered punchline to an unamused
audience. Through hopes, prayers, and some luck, this robot CAN score, and CAN play some mean defense. Most of the
time, it'll resort to unexpected intercourse with the ball, wall, other robot, or all of the above.


## SETUP
The values needed to be changed for the bot are:
CAMERA FOV: 1.57
CAMERA HEIGHT: 1px
CAMERA WIDTH: 1024px
